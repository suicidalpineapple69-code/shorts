## Posting
- **Post now.** Halloween searches and browsing peak in the last week of October.
- Post about 2 hours before your viewers' peak time (YouTube Studio → Analytics → Audience → *When your viewers are on YouTube*). For a mostly US teen audience that's around 3–6 pm local.
- Reply to as many comments as you can in the first hour.
- Don't delete and re-upload if it starts slow. Shorts often get picked up a day or two later.
- **Audience setting:** answer the "Made for kids" question honestly. Note that *Made for kids* turns comments off, which removes the "comment 1–4" hook.
- Everything in the video is original: rendered world, music, sound effects and fonts with open licenses. The organ sting is Bach's public-domain Toccata motif, played by a synth. The narrator is an AI voice (Kokoro TTS). This should be safe from Content ID claims.
